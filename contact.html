Frequently Asked Contact Questions

What information should I include when reporting an issue?

When reporting a technical issue, please include:

  • Your device type and operating system
  • Browser name and version
  • A detailed description of the issue
  • Steps to reproduce the problem (if applicable)
  • Screenshots or screen recordings (if possible)

This helps us diagnose and resolve your issue more quickly.

How long does it take to get a response?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 1-2 business days. For urgent issues, we try to respond even faster. Please note that response times may be longer during weekends and holidays.

How can I suggest a new feature?

We love hearing your ideas! The best way to suggest a new feature is to email us at feedback@easycount.app with "Feature Request" in the subject line. Please describe the feature in detail and explain how it would benefit users.

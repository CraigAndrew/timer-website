About Easy Count

Our Story

Easy Count was born from a simple need: a clean, intuitive timer that works across devices without unnecessary complications. What started as a personal project by our founder in 2020 has grown into a trusted timing tool used by thousands of people worldwide for a variety of purposes.

We noticed that many existing timer applications were either too simplistic or overwhelmingly complex. Easy Count aims to strike the perfect balance—offering powerful functionality with an interface that anyone can use immediately without a learning curve.

Since our launch, we've been continuously improving Easy Count based on user feedback, adding features like different timer types, customizable alerts, and theme options while maintaining the simplicity that users love.

Our Mission

At Easy Count, our mission is to help people make the most of their time through intuitive, reliable timing tools. We believe that effective time management is essential for productivity, wellness, and balance in our fast-paced world.

Our core values include:

  • Simplicity: We design with simplicity at the forefront, ensuring our tools are accessible to everyone.
  • Reliability: We understand that people depend on our timer for important tasks, so reliability is non-negotiable.
  • Accessibility: We're committed to making Easy Count work for everyone, regardless of device, browser, or connection status.
  • Continuous Improvement: We're always refining and enhancing our application based on user feedback and emerging technologies.

How Easy Count Works

Unlike many web applications, Easy Count is designed to work efficiently without requiring constant server communication. Here's how we've built it:

Progressive Web App (PWA)

Easy Count is built as a Progressive Web App, meaning it loads quickly and can work offline once you've visited the site. You can even add it to your home screen on mobile devices for quick access.

Responsive Design

Our interface automatically adapts to different screen sizes, from small smartphones to large desktop monitors, providing an optimal experience on any device.

Local Storage

Your timer preferences and settings are saved locally on your device, meaning they persist between sessions without requiring an account or login.

Web Audio API

We use modern web technologies like the Web Audio API to provide clear, customizable alert sounds when your timer completes.
